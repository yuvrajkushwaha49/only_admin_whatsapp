# Dashboard Analytics Overview

## Analytics Dashboard
The **Analytics** module (`/analytics`) gives comprehensive insight into your messaging performance, audience growth, and campaign ROI:
- **Overview Tab:** High-level KPIs including total messages dispatched, delivery rate, open rate, active contacts, and response rate.
- **Campaigns Tab:** Comparative analysis across recent broadcast campaigns.
- **Contacts Tab:** Growth trends of opted-in subscribers vs. unsubscribed contacts.
- **Revenue / ROI Tab:** Track conversions and attribution from marketing broadcasts.
- **Date Range Filters:** Filter data by Today, Yesterday, Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days, This Month, or Custom Date Range.

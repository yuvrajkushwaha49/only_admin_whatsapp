# Subscription Plans and Tiers

## SaaS Plans Overview
The platform offers tiered subscription plans tailored for businesses of varying message volumes:
- **Starter Plan:** Entry-level package for small businesses starting out with WhatsApp marketing. Includes 1 WhatsApp number connection, up to 1,000 active contacts, and essential broadcasting.
- **Growth / Pro Plan:** Ideal for scaling brands. Includes higher messaging throughput, multiple agent seats, advanced audience segmentation, scheduled campaigns, and automated replies.
- **Enterprise Plan:** Dedicated high-volume sending, custom webhook integrations, unlimited agent seats, priority Meta support, and dedicated account management.

## Plan Upgrades & Modifications
Users can view current subscription status or upgrade plans directly from **Billing** (`/billing`).

# Audience Segmentation & Contact Groups

## Segmentation Strategy
Segmenting your contact list enables higher conversion rates and minimizes unsubscribe rates by sending relevant offers to targeted demographics.

## Ways to Segment
1. **Tags:** Flexible color-coded tags attached to contacts. A single contact can have multiple tags (e.g. `['Loyal Customer', 'Delhi NCR', 'Festival Buyer']`).
2. **Contact Groups:** Dedicated lists organizing contacts into static or dynamic cohorts (e.g., "Q4 Beta Testers", "Delhi Retailers").
3. **Opt-in Filters:** Isolate contacts who have actively opted in vs. those who requested opt-out.

## Using Segments in Campaigns
When launching a broadcast in **Campaign Builder**, you can select one or more Tags or Contact Groups. The system automatically deduplicates phone numbers so recipients belonging to multiple target groups only receive one message.

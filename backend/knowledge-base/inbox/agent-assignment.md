# Agent Routing and Conversation Assignment

## Routing Conversations
To ensure fast resolution times, conversations can be routed to specific agents:
- **Manual Assignment:** Admins or team leads can assign open conversations to any active agent.
- **Self-Assignment:** Agents can claim unassigned customer tickets from the common queue.
- **Filtering by Agent:** Agents can filter their inbox view to see only chats assigned to them ("My Chats") or view the overall company queue.
- **Transferring Chats:** An agent can seamlessly transfer a conversation to a specialist or supervisor along with internal handover notes.

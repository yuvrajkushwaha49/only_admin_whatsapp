# Live Chat & 24-Hour Customer Care Window

## Meta 24-Hour Service Window Rule
When a customer sends your WhatsApp Business number an inbound message, Meta opens a **free-form 24-hour customer service window**:
- **Inside the 24-hour window:** Agents can send standard, non-templated text messages, media files, and interactive options at zero extra messaging template fee.
- **Outside the 24-hour window (Window Closed):** Free-form text cannot be sent. You must send an approved Meta Template (e.g., Utility or Marketing template) to re-engage the customer. Once the customer replies to your template, a new 24-hour customer service window opens.

# Team Inbox Overview

## What is Team Inbox?
The **Team Inbox** (`/inbox`) provides a collaborative, multi-agent customer support chat interface for managing live two-way customer conversations on WhatsApp.

## Key Features
- **Multi-Agent Collaboration:** Multiple team members can respond to incoming customer chats simultaneously from one centralized WhatsApp number.
- **Real-Time Live Updates:** Powered by Socket.IO for instantaneous message delivery and typing indicators.
- **Conversation States:** Categorize chats into `Open`, `Pending`, or `Resolved/Closed`.
- **Private Internal Notes:** Leave internal comments on customer chats visible only to your team, not sent to the customer.
- **Rich Media & Quick Replies:** Send images, documents, audio clips, and pre-saved canned response snippets.

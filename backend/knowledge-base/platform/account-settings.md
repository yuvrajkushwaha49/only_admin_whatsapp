# Account and Workspace Settings

## Account Settings Overview
From the **Settings** page (`/settings`), users can configure:
- **Business Profile:** Business name, timezone (e.g. `Asia/Kolkata`), currency, and logo.
- **Notification Preferences:** Email and in-app alerts for low wallet balance, failed broadcasts, or critical Meta quality rating downgrades.
- **Security & Password:** Update account password, review active user sessions, and enable security protections.

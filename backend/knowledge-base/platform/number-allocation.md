# Number Allocation & Multi-Tenant Management

## Number Allocation Architecture
In multi-tenant SaaS environments, Super Admins can manage and allocate WhatsApp phone numbers to specific client businesses:
- **Allocation via Super Admin Suite:** Super Admins navigate to **Number Allocation** (`/admin/numbers`) to allot approved WhatsApp numbers to verified client tenants.
- **Tenant Isolation:** Once allotted, only the authorized client business can send messages, view inbox chats, or launch campaigns from that phone number.
- **Multi-Number Support:** A single business workspace can have multiple phone numbers (e.g., Sales line, Customer Support line) with separate assigned team members.

# Marketing Message Templates

## Definition & Use Cases
Marketing templates are business-initiated messages designed to promote products, announce sales offers, re-engage customers, or share promotional newsletters.

## Key Rules & Structure
- **Approval Required:** Meta must review and approve all marketing templates before they can be sent.
- **Header:** Optional text (up to 60 characters) or rich media (Image, Video, Document).
- **Body:** Up to 1,024 characters. Supports variable placeholders like `{{1}}`, `{{2}}`.
- **Footer:** Optional text up to 60 characters. Recommended for opt-out instructions (e.g., "Reply STOP to unsubscribe").
- **Buttons:**
  - Quick Reply: Up to 3 customizable buttons (e.g. "Interested", "Talk to Agent", "Unsubscribe").
  - Call to Action: Visit Website (dynamic or static URL) and Call Phone Number.

## Avoiding Meta Rejections
1. Ensure sample values are provided for all dynamic variables during submission (e.g. for `{{1}}`, provide "John").
2. Do not use abusive, misleading, or sensationalist content.
3. Choose the correct category (submitting promotional content as Utility leads to immediate rejection or category reassignment).

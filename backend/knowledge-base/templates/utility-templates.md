# Utility Message Templates

## Definition & Use Cases
Utility templates relate directly to an existing transaction, user request, or service update. They are typically lower cost than marketing conversations.

## Examples of Utility Messages
- Order confirmation and invoice receipts.
- Shipping updates, courier tracking numbers, and delivery notifications.
- Appointment reminders and booking updates.
- Account status changes or subscription renewal receipts.
- Password change confirmations (non-OTP).

## Strict Meta Restrictions
- Utility templates must **NOT** contain marketing, upsell, cross-sell, or discount language.
- Including phrases like "Use coupon SAVE20 for your next order" in a shipping notification will cause Meta to reject the template or classify it as Marketing.
- Meta conducts automated content auditing; if a template classified as Utility is detected sending promotional copy, Meta may reclassify it and retroactively adjust billing rates.

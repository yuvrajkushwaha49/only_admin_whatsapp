# Phone Number Verification & Display Name Guidelines

## Verification Process
To activate a phone number on Meta's WhatsApp Cloud API, Meta requires two levels of verification:
1. **Ownership Verification (OTP):** During Embedded Signup, a 6-digit verification code is delivered via SMS or Voice Call to verify ownership of the physical SIM or landline.
2. **Display Name Approval:** Meta reviews the chosen display name to ensure compliance with WhatsApp Commerce and Business Policies.

## WhatsApp Display Name Rules
- **Direct Business Association:** The display name must match your legal business name, brand trademark, or domain name (e.g., "Aiveexa Tech" or "TechSky Solutions").
- **No Generic Terms:** Names cannot be generic categories such as "Shoes", "Best Deals", or "Delhi Fashion".
- **Proper Capitalization:** Must not use all caps (except for established acronyms like NASA) or irregular capitalization.
- **No Emoticons or Symbols:** Do not include emojis, trademark symbols, or punctuation in display names.
- **Consistent Branding:** The name must be easily verifiable via your public business website or Meta Business verification documents.

## Green Tick (Official Business Account)
An Official Business Account (OBA) displays a green checkmark badge next to the business name in chat headers:
- Requires verified Meta Business Manager.
- High brand search volume and organic media notability.
- Application can be submitted via Meta Business Manager -> WhatsApp Accounts -> Phone Numbers -> Profile.
